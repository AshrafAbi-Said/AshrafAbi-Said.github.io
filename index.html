Hi! I’m Ashraf:
|

I’m a developer and designer with experience in both mobile (Falafel Games) and PC (A Heavy Morning, Nifty Craft) projects, spanning genres such as puzzle, party, co-op, MMO, educational, and story-driven games. I’m currently working on A Heavy Morning, a game you can check out on Steam!

Beyond development, I serve as a program coordinator for the Saudi Game Champions program, supporting dozens of teams as they build their games, refine their pitches, and learn directly from industry veterans. As a consultant, I’ve partnered with Ithra to provide design and testing feedback, helping PC and mobile titles cross the finish line.

I’ve also co-founded the Gaming Academy and the Gamedev Initiative for the Global South (GIGS) to raise the standard of game development across the MENA region. Currently, I’m the Treasurer of Arabic Games and Regional Coordinator for the IGDA Incubation SIG, continuing to support the growth of our developer community.

Over the years, I’ve spoken at conferences including Devcom, Pocket Gamer, PathfindinGS, and Zero2Hero, often focusing on themes of community, culture, and incubation/acceleration.

Outside of games, I volunteer at my family’s wild animal shelter, Animal Encounter, where we care for injured wildlife and teach visitors about the importance of conservation.

If you’re interested in consulting, freelance collaborations, speaking engagements, or just want to connect, feel free to reach out below!
